About

A fictional election simulation built for classrooms.

What this is

Every candidate you build or pick is invented. No real names or likenesses are used. It is a way to see how a candidate's background, message and personality might shape the way voters respond.

Your data

No account, no sign-up. Candidates save in your own browser. When you run a simulation, the profile you wrote is sent to an AI model and the report comes back. Nothing else is collected.

Fairness

No party, ideology or issue is treated as better or more electable. What gets scored is how well the candidate is written: clear message, believable background, memorable slogan, consistent story.
