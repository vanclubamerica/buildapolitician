How it works

Your candidate is scored on how well they are built, not on what they believe.

The steps

  1. You fill in eight fields. The description does most of the work.
  2. Your browser sends the profile to a server, which adds a secret key and forwards it to an AI model.
  3. The AI returns approval numbers, polling, voter groups, strengths, weaknesses and campaign events.
  4. The server checks the percentages add to 100 before anything is shown.

What moves the numbers

  • Clarity. Is the message specific, or could it belong to anyone?
  • Credibility. Does the old job support the issue? A nurse running on healthcare starts ahead.
  • Relatability. Would a normal voter recognize this person's life?
  • Memorability. Can someone repeat the slogan from memory?
  • Coherence. Do the age, job, state, issue and personality tell one story?

Party never changes the score

No party or issue is treated as more popular or more electable than another. A party label changes which voter groups start friendly, and nothing else. Two equally well written candidates score the same no matter what they believe.

This is tested, not just promised. Change only a candidate's party and the headline number does not move.

If the AI is unavailable

A built-in offline model produces a full report using the same rules, so the page always works. It tells you when that happens.

This is fiction

Every voter, poll and event is invented. Real polling surveys thousands of actual people and reports a margin of error. This is a teaching tool for learning the parts of a campaign, not a way to predict anything.
